Type: Georgia 16/25 for body; PP Eiko Medium for headings (h1 36/42, 30/36 below 600px; h2 24/30; h3 20/26), navigation and the footer (15px); Helvetica Bold for the alphabet and `ez._` logo; Fira Code for code. All fonts are self-hosted.

## Layout

- Reading pages share one layout (`reading_start` and `reading_end` in `build.sh`): a 720px column with 24px gutters, masthead (logo + `about ~ blog ~ contents ~ changelog`, no underlines at rest; after the first ericzheng.nz, hovering a link grows a 1.5px red line out from its middle, just below the descenders, over 250ms, instantly with reduced motion), content with 24px gaps, and a footer with GitHub/LinkedIn/Strava icons and “back to top ↑”. Below 600px the masthead stacks.
- About: 160px portrait (96px on mobile) beside the intro, with the next three photos fanned behind it, turning on its bottom edge (−7°, 7°, 14°; a little wider on hover or focus; leaning into the column gap below 840px and on mobile) over the caption “click to see more photos”. Clicking it opens a paper sheet of every photo in loose, tilted rows in list order, the portrait first and a little larger; each photo’s size, nudge and tilt come from its file name, and the sheet scrolls when they outgrow the screen. Picking a photo brings it forward, straight and large, with its caption on a slip over its corner; bare paper puts it back or closes the sheet, which fades out over 200ms. Paper page **Planning · About · photo fan & scatter**. Then “fun fact:” and “about this site:”, each a section with a 20/26 heading below a hairline `--rule` top border (8px of margin, 32px of padding above it, 28px on mobile) and 22px between the paragraphs inside it. The yearbook quote in the fun fact is italic with a 2px rule on its left in `--link` at 45% (`#7c414773`) and 20px of indent. Links in the lists and caption look like every other link.
- Blog and Leetcode: compact list — italic date right-aligned in a 132px column, 18px Eiko title, an italic line under it (the summary on Blog, the difficulty on Leetcode), 28px between posts. Dates stack above titles on mobile.
- Leetcode difficulty: a 2px rule in the difficulty's colour runs down each entry's title and difficulty, 10px from the text — Easy `#1cb8b8`, Medium `#ffb800`, Hard `--accent` (one red on the page, not LeetCode's near-miss `#f63636`). It takes 12px of the 24px gap beside the date, so titles stay where Blog's are; below 600px it runs down the whole entry, date included, indenting it 14px. The word stays ink: the bright colours are too pale for text on the paper (amber is 1.5:1). Paper page **Audit · Leetcode · difficulty colour**: board 00 compares six routes, the rule is route 3.
- Changelog: dated entries without titles or rules — a 132px muted date column beside each summary, 32px between entries; dates stack above the summary below 600px. As many entries a page as fit, with the index pagination row below.
- Index pagination (Paper **Audit · Pagination & jump to page**): after an index's last entry, a row at least 44px tall, all on one baseline — `← previous` left and `next →` right as Eiko 15/22 links, in equal-width ends so the middle stays centred; with enlarged text the row wraps rather than spills. The middle reads `8 / 23` in muted Georgia 16/24, the 8 being a field you can type a page into: the current page as grey hint text over a 1px `--muted` underline (the paper rule is too faint for a field), sized for the last page's digits and set below the old-style figures' tails, inside a 44px label. Hovering darkens the line to ink; focus turns it 2px `--accent`, like the masthead's hover line, instead of the usual ring. Enter, or a phone keyboard's Go key, opens that page; there is no button. With JavaScript (`public/js/page-jump.js`) the jump happens in place, a key that would make an impossible page is ignored (after 2, a 4 does nothing on a 23-page index; so are letters, signs and a leading 0), and Escape or clicking away puts the current page back. Without it, a plain form sends `?page=N` to `/<collection>/page`, which `vercel.json` redirects, and the browser's own range check applies. An inactive end is muted text, not focusable; the whole row is omitted when the list fits one page. An index page holds as many entries as fit on the screen with the pagination row and the space below it, the footer being free to sit below the fold; never fewer than 5 on Blog, Leetcode and Changelog (`PAGE_SIZE` in `build.sh`) or 10 on Books (`BOOKS_PAGE_SIZE`), so a phone, or a laptop under about 900px tall, shows the fewest and scrolls. `public/js/page-fit.js` works the pages out from the first entry when an index opens, so previous and next never skip or repeat an entry, and a page number past the last moves to the last; resizing the window takes effect on the next page. The script runs in the page's head, before anything is drawn, and keeps the list and the pagination row invisible until it has fitted them, so a tall screen never shows the fewest first and then jumps, in any browser. Without JavaScript every page holds the fewest. Page 1 stays `/blog`, `/leetcode`, `/changelog` and `/books`, later pages are `/blog/page/2`, `/leetcode/page/2`, `/changelog/page/2` and `/books/page/2`, titled “Leetcode, page 2”.
- Contents: two-column directories (132px, or 252px for emails) of internal pages, social profiles and emails, stacking on mobile; the external links sit below a hairline rule. Links keep 44px tap targets.
- Friends: one Contents directory of friends' websites, each with a note, under the intro; not in the masthead, so no link is current.
- Comments, under each blog post (Paper **Planning · Comments**, draft C: C1 / C2 desktop and mobile, C4 / C7 the not-sent page, C5 every state; A and B are the drafts not picked. After a send is on page **Audit · Comments · after sending**, A and C; it replaced C3 / C6): a section below a hairline `--rule` with 32px above, headed “comments:” (h2) with the count, “4 so far”, in muted italic 14/22 at the right once they've loaded. Each comment is the name in Eiko 18/26 and the date in muted italic 14/22 on one baseline, 10px apart, then the text in Georgia 16/25 4px below, its line breaks kept and a blank line starting a paragraph 12px on; 28px between comments. Eric's are indented 24px (14px on mobile) on the yearbook quote's 2px rule in `--link` at 45%, with 20px (16px) inside. While loading, with none yet, when they couldn't load (with a “try again” link) and without JavaScript, one muted Georgia 16/25 line stands in for the list. After a send, “sent:” (h3), the thanks and a “leave another” link take the form's place, shown by `:target` (the link brings the form back); with JavaScript, the reader's own comment also ends the list in `--muted`, with “waiting for me to read it” where the date goes, until Eric has approved it. The form follows 20px lower: “leave one:” (h3) with a muted 14/22 note 4px under it; name and email side by side 24px apart (stacked below 600px), then the comment; labels in Eiko 15/22, 6px above fields on `#f7f6f2`, 40px tall (44px on mobile), the comment 140px and taller if dragged; a 1px `--muted` underline that darkens to ink on hover and turns 2px `--accent` on focus or when the browser refuses the field. “never shown” sits in muted italic at the right of the email label. “send” is an ink button with paper-coloured Eiko 15/22, 44px tall, full width on mobile; hovering underlines the word in 1.5px `--accent`. A send that fails lands on /comment-not-sent, a plain reading page.
- Birthday (`/birthday-2026`, unlisted): the details and the menu in Contents' 132px column, but with the label muted and the value ink; a choice of two dishes shows both under one label, with a muted italic *or* between them. The calendar buttons are 44px tall, with a 1px ink border and Eiko 15/22 text; hovering underlines the words in 1.5px `--accent`. Below 600px they stack at full width. The menu and the guests are sections below a hairline, like About's notes. The guests run alphabetically left to right in four 132px columns, two on mobile. Not in the masthead, so no link is current.
- Books: a reading log, newest first, built like the Blog list: the day each book was finished in the 132px muted italic date column, then the title as an Eiko 18/26 link (a button that opens the book), the author in muted italic and a `--link` ¶ when Eric wrote a review; 18px between books (20px on mobile, where the date sits above the title). Pointing at a title, or tabbing to it, slips its cover in at the column's right edge, 120px wide, standing on that line and tilted 3° (fading and lifting over 200ms); not below 600px. Clicking it opens a dialog: paper at 72% over the page, the cover straight and up to 492px tall, and a `#f7f6f2` slip tilted −2° over its right edge near the foot, holding the title (Eiko 20/26), author, date and the review under a hairline. Below 780px the slip sits under the cover. Close ×, bare paper or Escape puts it back, fading out over 200ms (`public/js/fade-out.js`). As many books a page as fit, and at least ten, with the index pagination: `/books`, then `/books/page/2`. Not in the masthead, so no link is current.
- Edge waves: on every page, a fixed column of ASCII characters sits in each side gutter (14px on phones and tablets, widening from about 1000px to 296px at 1440 and at most 360px; at most 204px on Home, and narrower on very small Home screens to clear the alphabet). The characters change in place about 12 times a second; the field never moves. The right wave shows how far you've read: ink above, a red band at the current position, pale below; pages that don't scroll are fully inked (paper-coloured on Home's dark half). The left wave is always `#c8c5bc` and is not a mirror of the right. With reduced motion or no JavaScript, a still image (`public/waves/`) is shown; print and forced-colors hide both.
- Home: the split alphabet. Hovering or focusing a linked row spreads its letters (0 → 0.15em over 280ms) while the link stays put, once the opening has finished (a pointer already resting on a link eases it open then); off on touch, below 600px and with reduced motion.
- Home opening, after the first ericzheng.nz (1.5s, CSS only): the page starts as the plain alphabet in `#666`, the grey it keeps under the curtain. After 150ms a dark curtain falls top down (600ms ease-in-out), lighting the four links; after 150ms in the dark its left half lifts bottom up (600ms ease-out), turning ERIC/ZHENG red. Each moving edge is a 2px red line, and the waves appear as the curtain passes. Two `aria-hidden` copies of the alphabet are clipped away over the real one, so the links work throughout. It plays whenever Home is shown; reduced motion, print and forced colors skip it.

## Kept for later

- **Highlighter stroke.** A soft stroke of a colour behind a word, from mid x-height to just under the baseline, with the word left in ink (`linear-gradient(transparent 50%, <colour> 50% 84%, transparent 84%)` at 22–42% opacity). Eric likes it; it was tried on the Leetcode difficulty and left out for now. Boards 01–02 on the **Audit · Leetcode · difficulty colour** page show it with the rule, 03–05 add alternating `#f6f5f1` shades. Mind that a yellow stroke looks like the `#ffd987` selection.

## Working in Paper

- Inspect the file and confirm scope before editing. For a new exploration, add a new, clearly named page instead of changing Page 1.
- Duplicate boards to an archive page before destructive edits.
- Check desktop and mobile boards for clipping and overlap after edits.
- Keep Eric's words: don't paraphrase his copy or invent taglines, descriptions or summaries.

The animated field adapts the wave-domain and ordered-dithering mathematics from:
https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/the-art-of-dithering-and-retro-shading-web/
https://github.com/maximeheckel/blog.maximeheckel.com/blob/main/core/components/MDX/Widgets/Retro/waves.ts

The Classic Perlin noise implementation derives from:
https://github.com/stegu/webgl-noise/blob/master/src/classicnoise2D.glsl

The right static PNG fallbacks are the owner's Paper light-biased Helvetica ASCII studies.
The left PNG masks are independent time8 samples of the local field, drawn with the
site's Helvetica font by scripts/generate-left-wave-masks.mjs (not reflected images).
The ASCII/light-bias/edge/progress adaptation and CPU canvas renderer are local to this site.
